# Anti AI Slop Design Notes

Use this checklist before each visual pass on this site.

## Avoided Patterns

- Do not use Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk, Roboto, Arial, or default system-font stacks as the primary typeface.
- Avoid purple, violet, and indigo gradient defaults.
- Avoid gradient text, neon-on-dark styling, decorative glows, and spotlight halos unless the content truly needs them.
- Avoid generic three-card rows with identical icon-card treatment. If cards are used, make them serve a real interaction or content pattern.
- Avoid beige or cream surfaces as the default "tasteful" answer.
- Avoid oversized rounded rectangles, nested cards, and decorative shadows that do not clarify hierarchy.
- Avoid polished UI that implies interactions that are not implemented.
- Keep typography, spacing, and imagery tied to the medical/pulmonology context instead of generic SaaS styling.

## Current Direction

- Typeface: Afacad, chosen as a clean humanist sans with a softer medical feel and less AI-template association than Inter/Geist/Space Grotesk.
- Palette: clinical white, oxygen teal, faint blue-green surfaces, and controlled coral accents from the lung imagery.
- Surfaces: use light blue-green paneling for second-section focus areas instead of plain white-on-white or beige.
- Motion: scroll progress should explain the "before to after" story, not exist as decorative animation.

## Research Sources

- Impeccable: https://impeccable.style/slop and https://impeccable.style/changelog
  Notes overused fonts including Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk, Instrument Serif, Fraunces, Mona Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, Recoleta, and Instrument Sans; also flags purple/violet gradients, glow-heavy dark modes, gradient text, and cream/beige defaults.
- 925 Studios: https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-design-tells
  Identifies Inter, indigo-to-purple gradients, rounded three-card rows, and generic hero layouts as recurring AI design tells.
- Business Insider, July 2026: https://www.businessinsider.com/ai-coded-app-user-interface-experience-design-2026-7
  Describes AI-coded UI convergence toward generic sans-serif typography, muted palettes, rounded corners, shadows, "pretty but dysfunctional" happy-path screens, and weak edge-case handling.
- The New Yorker, June 24, 2026: https://www.newyorker.com/culture/infinite-scroll/the-ai-design-aesthetic-thats-taking-over-the-internet
  Describes repeated AI-design aesthetics such as beige/cream surfaces, rusty accents, large serif emphasis, tracked-out subheads, repeated rounded outlines, and generic sameness across outputs.
